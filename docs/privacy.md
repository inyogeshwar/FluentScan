# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Overview

FluentScan is designed with your privacy in mind. This document explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

**Local Data Only:**
- Scanned documents stored locally on your device
- App settings stored in AppData
- Diagnostic logs for error reporting

**Optional Data:**
- Anonymous usage analytics via App Center (can be disabled)
- Crash reports when app fails

### What We Don't Collect

- No cloud storage of your documents
- No transmission of document content
- No personal identification unless you provide it
- No advertising or marketing tracking

## Data Usage

### Diagnostics
We use anonymous crash reports to:
- Fix bugs and errors
- Improve app stability
- Understand feature usage

### Storage
All your scanned documents remain on your device:
- Default: `Documents\FluentScan`
- Customizable in settings
- Only you can access

## Third Parties

### App Center (Microsoft)
- Provides crash analytics
- No personal data collected
- Can be disabled in settings

### Windows APIs
- Uses Windows built-in scanner APIs
- Follows Windows privacy guidelines
- No additional data shared

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Delete all app data (uninstall removes all)
- Disable optional analytics
- Export your settings
- Request data deletion (contact: yogeshwar853202@gmail.com)

## Security

- No network transmission of documents
- Local storage only
- No authentication required
- Standard Windows file permissions

## Children

FluentScan is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
