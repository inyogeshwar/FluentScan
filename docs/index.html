A feature-rich scanning application for Windows 10 and Windows 11. Scan documents to PDF, merge pages, and organize your scans with a clean, intuitive interface.

Features

Everything you need to digitize your documents

📷

Universal Scanner Support

Works with most Windows-compatible WIA scanners. Automatic scanner discovery makes it easy to get started.

📄

Multiple Formats

Save scans as PDF, JPEG, PNG, or TIFF. Create single or multi-page PDFs with the merge feature.

🎨

Modern Design

Windows 11 Fluent Design with Mica effect. Supports dark and light themes with adaptive layouts.

🌍

Multi-language

Available in 25 languages. The app automatically uses your system language.

⚙️

Customizable

Configure default scan settings, save locations, and file naming patterns to streamline your workflow.

🔒

Privacy Focused

All documents stay on your device. No cloud storage or document uploads. Your data never leaves your PC.
