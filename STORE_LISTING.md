## Long Description

### Overview
FluentScan is a modern, feature-rich scanning application for Windows 10 and Windows 11. With support for most WIA-compatible scanners, it provides a clean and efficient way to digitize your documents.

### Key Features
- **Universal Scanner Support**: Works with most Windows-compatible scanners
- **Multiple Formats**: Save scans as PDF, JPEG, PNG, or TIFF
- **Page Merging**: Combine multiple scans into a single PDF
- **Custom Naming**: Use patterns to automatically name your files
- **Modern Design**: Windows 11 Fluent Design with Mica effect
- **Multi-language**: Available in 25 languages

### Scanning Options
- Color, grayscale, and black & white modes
- Resolution from 100 to 600 DPI
- Automatic document detection
- Auto-rotate for scanned pages
- Brightness and contrast adjustment

### Organization
- Configure your save location
- Quick access to recent scans
- Batch scanning support
- Custom file naming patterns

### Privacy
Your documents stay on your device. FluentScan doesn't upload your scans to any server. See our privacy policy for details.
