# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-05-13

## Summary

FluentScan respects your privacy. This app stores all data locally and does not transmit your scanned documents to any external servers.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

**Local Only:**
- Scanned documents stored in your chosen location
- App settings stored in AppData
- Configuration files

**Optional:**
- Anonymous usage analytics (can be disabled)
- Crash reports

### What We Don't Collect
- No cloud storage of documents
- No transmission of document content
- No personal information (unless you provide)
- No advertising tracking

## Data Usage

### Local Storage
All scanned documents remain on your device:
- Default: `Documents\FluentScan`
- Configurable in settings
- Only accessible by your account

### Analytics
Anonymous analytics help improve the app:
- Usage patterns
- Crash information
- Feature usage statistics

You can disable analytics in Settings > Privacy.

## Third-Party Services

### Microsoft App Center
- Provides crash reporting
- No personal data
- Can be disabled

### Windows
- Uses Windows scanner APIs
- Follows Windows privacy policy

## Your Rights

- Delete all app data by uninstalling
- Disable optional analytics
- Request data deletion
- Export your settings
